I used to manage engineers. Then I realized the gap between what I could imagine and what I could explain to someone else was the constraint. So I closed it.

Now I co-found Dasein Foresight and ship the entire stack: landing, CRM, multi-agent simulation engine, AI OS, report layer. What you see in this portfolio is production infrastructure for a live company — not exercises.

The useful part is that I think in product terms and build in technical ones simultaneously — which means I never build the wrong thing precisely.

Also a DJ and generative art enthusiast. The shader in the hero above is mine.

Manejaba engineers. Después me di cuenta de que la brecha entre lo que podía imaginar y lo que podía explicarle a alguien más era la restricción. Así que la cerré.

Lo útil es que pienso en términos de producto y construyo en términos técnicos al mismo tiempo — lo que significa que nunca construyo la cosa equivocada con precisión.
